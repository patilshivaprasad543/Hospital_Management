How the Care Workflow Works

📅

Book

Patient books an appointment with a specialist doctor.

✅

Approve

Doctor reviews and confirms the appointment request.

🎟️

Check-In

Patient checks in digitally and receives a queue ticket.

🩺

Consult

Doctor conducts consultation and creates prescriptions.

🔬

Lab

Diagnostic tests are processed by lab vendors.

💊

Pharmacy

Medicines are ordered and fulfilled by pharmacy vendors.

🏁

Complete

Care episode is wrapped up with full medical records.

👤 For Patients

See real-time progress on every appointment — know exactly what step you're on and what action to take next.

👨‍⚕️ For Doctors

Monitor your active patient queue with a clear view of each case's workflow stage.

🏪 For Vendors

Lab and pharmacy vendors receive notifications when patients reach their step in the workflow.
