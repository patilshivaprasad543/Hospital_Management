About SmartCare 360

Transforming hospital operations with intelligent, connected healthcare technology

Our Mission

SmartCare 360 was built to eliminate the fragmentation in hospital workflows. We connect every stakeholder — patients seeking care, doctors delivering it, labs running diagnostics, and pharmacies dispensing medicines — through a single, secure digital platform.

Our goal is to reduce waiting times, improve clinical outcomes, and give healthcare providers the tools they need to focus on what matters most: patient care.

What We Offer

  • Role-based portals for Admin, Patient, Doctor, Lab Vendor, and Pharmacy
  • OTP-verified registration with admin approval workflows
  • Smart appointment calendar with real-time slot management
  • Digital prescriptions, lab requests, and pharmacy orders
  • Billing, invoicing, and PDF report generation
  • Email and WhatsApp notifications across all workflows
  • Comprehensive audit logs and department management
