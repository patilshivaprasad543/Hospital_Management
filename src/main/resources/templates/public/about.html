About SmartCare 360

A complete hospital management ecosystem built for modern healthcare delivery in India

Who We Are

SmartCare 360 is an integrated Hospital Management System (HMS) designed to digitize end-to-end hospital operations — from patient registration and doctor appointments to laboratory diagnostics, pharmacy dispensing, billing, and administrative oversight.

Founded with a vision to make quality healthcare accessible and efficient, SmartCare 360 serves hospitals, multi-specialty clinics, diagnostic centers, and pharmacy chains across India. Our platform replaces paper-based workflows with a secure, role-based digital system that connects every stakeholder in the care journey.

Our Mission

To simplify hospital administration, reduce patient waiting times, and empower healthcare professionals with intelligent tools — so they can spend more time caring for patients and less time on paperwork.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted digital healthcare platform, enabling every hospital — from tier-1 metros to district health centers — to deliver connected, transparent, and patient-centric care.

Platform Modules

Patient Management

Online registration with OTP verification, health profile (blood group, allergies, emergency contacts), appointment booking, medical timeline, prescription history, lab reports, and invoice downloads.

Doctor & Consultation

Doctor profiles with specialization and fees, availability scheduling, live patient queue, digital consultations (symptoms, diagnosis, treatment), e-prescriptions, and lab test requests.

Administration

Central admin dashboard with analytics, doctor/vendor approval workflows, department management, user management, appointment oversight, and full audit log tracking.

Lab & Pharmacy

Vendor portals for diagnostic labs (test catalog, report uploads) and pharmacies (inventory management, prescription order fulfillment) with real-time status updates.

Billing & Payments

Automated invoice generation after consultations, online payment processing, PDF invoice downloads, and revenue tracking for hospital administrators.

Notifications

Multi-channel alerts via in-app notifications, email, and WhatsApp for appointments, OTP verification, approvals, prescriptions, lab reports, and billing updates.

How It Works

1

Register & Verify

Patients, doctors, and vendors register through role-specific portals. Email OTP verification ensures account security. Doctors and vendors require admin document approval.

2

Book & Consult

Patients browse doctors by department, book available time slots, and check in for appointments. Doctors manage their queue and conduct digital consultations.

3

Diagnose & Treat

Doctors issue digital prescriptions, order lab tests, and record treatment plans. Lab vendors upload reports; pharmacies fulfill medicine orders.

4

Bill & Follow Up

Invoices are generated automatically. Patients pay online and download PDF receipts. Follow-up appointments and notifications keep care continuous.
