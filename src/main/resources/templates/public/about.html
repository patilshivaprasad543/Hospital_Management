About SmartCare 360

A complete hospital management platform built for modern healthcare in India

Who We Are

SmartCare 360 is an integrated hospital management platform designed to simplify care for patients, doctors, laboratories, pharmacies, and hospital administrators.

We help hospitals and clinics move from paper-based workflows to a connected digital experience — from registration and appointments to prescriptions, lab reports, pharmacy orders, and billing.

Our Mission

To simplify hospital administration, reduce patient waiting times, and help healthcare professionals spend more time caring for patients.

Our Vision

To make quality digital healthcare accessible for hospitals and patients across India.

Patient Care

Register online, book appointments, view prescriptions, track lab reports, order medicines, and pay bills from one portal.

Doctor Consultations

Manage your schedule, see your patient queue, record consultations, issue prescriptions, and request lab tests.

Hospital Administration

Approve doctors and vendors, manage departments, oversee appointments, and publish hospital announcements.

Lab & Pharmacy

Billing & Payments

View invoices, pay online, and download receipts after consultations and services.

Stay informed with updates about appointments, approvals, prescriptions, lab reports, pharmacy orders, and payments.

How It Works

1

Register & Verify

Create your account, verify your email, and complete your profile. Doctors and vendors are reviewed by the hospital team.

2

Book & Consult

Patients choose a doctor, book an available slot, and attend the consultation in person or through the care workflow.

3

Diagnose & Treat

Doctors provide prescriptions and lab requests. Labs upload reports and pharmacies fulfill medicine orders.

4

Bill & Follow Up

Patients receive bills, make payments, and get follow-up reminders through the portal.
