Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register as a patient?

Visit the Patient Portal, click "Create Patient Account", fill in your details, and verify your email with the OTP sent to your inbox. Once verified, you can log in and book appointments.

How can a doctor join SmartCare 360?

Doctors register at the Doctor Portal, complete OTP verification, submit required documents (medical license, ID proof), and wait for admin approval. Once approved, you can set your schedule and start accepting patients.

Is my health data secure?

Yes. SmartCare 360 uses secure sign-in and limits access so only authorized people can view records relevant to their role.

Can hospitals request a full platform demo?

Absolutely. Email sales@smartcare360.com or use the contact form above with subject "Hospital Demo Request". Our team will schedule a walkthrough of all modules including admin, patient, doctor, lab, and pharmacy portals.

How will I receive updates?

You will see updates in your portal notification center and by email for appointments, approvals, prescriptions, lab reports, pharmacy orders, and billing.
