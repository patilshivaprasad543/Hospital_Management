# SmartCare 360 — Project Overview

**Published website:** [https://hospital-management-glt1-ge8d.onrender.com](https://hospital-management-glt1-ge8d.onrender.com)

This document describes the **SmartCare 360** hospital care portal as it is available online. It is written for readers, reviewers, and hospital users. It does not describe how the system is built.

---

## 1. What the project is

SmartCare 360 is a complete digital hospital platform. It brings patients, doctors, laboratories, pharmacies, and hospital administrators onto one website so care can move from paper files to a connected online experience.

People can register, book visits, receive consultation notes and prescriptions, request lab tests, order medicines, and follow bills — from a browser, without visiting a separate system for each step.

## 3. Who it is for

| Portal | Who uses it | What they do |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| **Patient** | People seeking care | Register, book doctors, view prescriptions and lab reports, order medicines, pay bills |
| **Doctor** | Practising clinicians | See the day’s patients, record consultations, write prescriptions, request tests |
| **Laboratory** | Diagnostic partners | Receive test requests, update results, share reports with the hospital |
| **Pharmacy** | Medicine partners | Receive prescription orders, update stock and delivery status |
| **Administrator** | Hospital management | Approve staff and partners, oversee appointments, billing, and announcements |

Each person signs in to **their own portal**. Patients do not see admin tools; doctors do not see pharmacy stock screens.

---

## 4. What patients can do

- Create an account and verify it by email  
- Book an appointment with a specialist (for example cardiology, neurology, paediatrics, general medicine)  
- Receive confirmation with doctor name, date, and time  
- Attend the visit record, read the consultation summary, and download documents when offered  
- View prescriptions written by the doctor  
- Request or track laboratory tests  
- Order medicines from the hospital pharmacy flow  
- See invoices and complete payment steps in the portal  
- Read hospital notices and personal notifications  

---

## 5. What doctors can do

- Sign in to a dedicated doctor portal  
- Review the appointment list and patient queue  
- Record consultation notes  
- Issue prescriptions  
- Request laboratory tests  
- Follow up on completed reports  
- Manage leave or availability where the hospital has enabled it  

---

## 6. Laboratory and pharmacy

**Laboratory**

- Receive test orders linked to a patient and doctor  
- Update progress and attach or enter results  
- Make reports available to the care team and the patient  

**Pharmacy**

- Receive medicine orders from prescriptions  
- Confirm stock and fulfilment  
- Move an order from placed through to delivered  
- Keep the patient informed through portal updates  

---

## 7. Hospital administration

Administrators use a private hospital portal to:

- Approve new doctors and vendor partners  
- Manage departments  
- Watch appointments, prescriptions, orders, and billing across the hospital  
- Publish announcements  
- Support safe, organised day-to-day operations  

Admin access is not published on the public home page.

---

## 8. Safety and communication

- Separate sign-in for each role  
- Email checks when a new person registers  
- Appointment and status updates by email where mail is configured  
- In-portal notifications for prescriptions, lab work, pharmacy, and bills  
- Documents such as appointment or order summaries can be downloaded as printable files when the hospital enables them  

Records entered on the live website are kept for later visits, including after the public site has been idle.
